# Research Note 06 — Conversational UX, vision, proactive behavior, privacy/security, operations, market context

Research date: 2026-09-11. Hard technical numbers were verified directly from GitHub source and docs (Home Assistant core, ESPHome, Frigate, Pipecat, LiveKit, OpenAI SDK, Gemini cookbook, Kyutai, MentraOS, Brilliant Labs, Omi, Supervisor). Vendor and press claims come from search-result summaries and are marked **(search summary)** or **(uncertain)**.

## 1. Conversational UX and latency

**Human baseline.** Turn-taking research puts the typical human response gap at ~200 ms; humans expect 200–300 ms gaps. Industry thresholds quoted consistently: above ~300 ms a reply "starts to feel wrong", above ~500 ms users consciously notice, above ~800 ms it feels delayed, above ~1,500 ms the conversation "feels broken" (Hamming AI, Ken Imoto, FutureAGI; search summaries).

**Component budgets (2026, search summaries).** TTS time-to-first-audio: Cartesia Sonic 3.5 75–90 ms over WebSocket (a "Sonic 4 ~40 ms" claim appeared in one summary and is **uncertain**); ElevenLabs Flash ~75–120 ms; Deepgram Aura-2 ~90 ms. Rule of thumb: if TTS TTFB exceeds ~200 ms, interruption/turn-taking gets clunky. Fully local: RealtimeTTS reports ~81 ms predicted audible onset with a Qwen engine on a tuned RTX 4090; Kyutai Unmute ~750 ms on one GPU falling to ~450 ms on separate GPUs (STT 2.5 GB, TTS 5.3 GB, LLM 6.1 GB VRAM); Moshi 160 ms theoretical / ~200 ms on an L4 with a 24 GB GPU.

**Endpointing defaults worth copying (verified in source):**
- Pipecat Silero VAD: confidence 0.7, start 0.2 s, stop 0.2 s; Smart Turn v3.2 invoked only after VAD silence on up to 8 s of audio, `stop_secs=3` fallback ceiling, 500 ms pre-speech padding; turn detected in ~200 ms, final transcript ~250 ms after forcing STT end-of-utterance (Daily blog).
- LiveKit Agents: semantic turn detector (<500 MB RAM, CPU); `max_endpointing_delay` 3.0 s; `user_away_timeout` 15 s; default TTS text transforms `filter_markdown` and `filter_emoji`.
- OpenAI Realtime: `server_vad` threshold 0.5, prefix padding 300 ms, silence 500 ms; `semantic_vad` eagerness low/medium/high/auto = 8 s/4 s/2 s/4 s max wait; `interrupt_response`, `idle_timeout_ms`.
- Home Assistant Assist pipeline: VAD silence 0.7 s default (relaxed 1.25 s, aggressive 0.25 s), speech onset 0.3 s, 15 s command timeout, wake-word timeout 3 s, wake-word cooldown 2 s, pipeline timeout 300 s.

**Streaming and acknowledgements.** Home Assistant streams LLM deltas to TTS once >60 characters have arrived or a tool call follows text (`STREAM_RESPONSE_CHARS = 60`); ESPHome satellites received a "fix pause before Assist response" in July 2026. OpenAI's realtime-agents reference implements a **chat-supervisor pattern**: a fast realtime model answers immediately and must always say a neutral filler ("Just a second", "Let me check") before calling a slower reasoning supervisor; the demo notes ~2 s between filler and substantive answer. The supervisor prompt: "be very concise, use prose, and never create bulleted lists".

**Persona/prompt patterns.** Home Assistant's default LLM instruction: "You are a voice assistant for Home Assistant. Answer questions about the world truthfully. Respond simply and to the point in plain text." Vendor guides converge on: plain text only (TTS will read "asterisk"), one to two sentences per turn, never more than three options, specify role/tone/pace, calibrate disfluency to persona.

**Interruption etiquette.** Voice PE ships a hidden "stop" wake model active only during TTS longer than 1 s, and ducks announcements 20 dB. Voice PE issues #621 (Aug 2026), #563/#537 (2026): in continued-conversation mode the mic reopens before the device's own TTS finishes and it transcribes itself. Design half-duplex gating tied to actual playback position (OpenAI's guide recommends a playback tracker for the same reason).

**Wake word vs follow-up mode.** HA's continued conversation is a heuristic: `continue_conversation` is true only when the assistant's message ends with "?", ";" or "？"; the device loops back to listening without a wake word, 300 s `conversation_timeout`. Google added Continued Conversation to Gemini for Home in April 2026. In August 2026 HA added a constraint-driven **"gazetteer" matcher** fallback ("fuzzy similarity is evidence, not permission") so fuzzy names and "turn them off" follow-ups resolve locally before any LLM.

**Jarvis expectations vs reality.** The film JARVIS is infallible and anticipatory; 2026 assistants converse, see and act but still produce confident errors and need confirmation for consequential actions. Budget for <500 ms voice-to-voice on local intents, <1 s to first audio for LLM turns via streaming plus a filler, and treat 2–3 s tool-backed answers as acceptable only if acknowledged.

## 2. Vision and multimodal

**Cloud live vision.** Gemini Live API cookbook (Sept 2026) uses `gemini-3.1-flash-live-preview`, sends camera or screen JPEG frames at 1 fps thumbnailed to 1024x1024, audio 16 kHz in / 24 kHz out, context-window compression (trigger 25,600 tokens, target 12,800); function calling, code execution, Google Search; async function-response scheduling (INTERRUPT / WHEN_IDLE / SILENT). OpenAI Realtime is GA with `input_image` items (PNG/JPEG data URI), remote MCP and SIP; it adds pictures to the conversation rather than streaming video, so you sample frames yourself.

**Local VLMs (verified from repos).** Qwen3-VL (Apache-2.0) shipped 235B-A22B, 30B-A3B, 4B/8B, 2B/32B (Sep–Oct 2025) with 32-language OCR and 256K context. Llama 4 Scout/Maverick natively multimodal (custom license). llama.cpp's multimodal server supports Gemma 3/4, SmolVLM 256M–2.2B, Qwen2.5-VL 3–72B, Pixtral, InternVL, Llama 4 Scout, Moondream2 via `--mmproj`. Frigate docs recommend qwen3-vl (best small-object detail), qwen3.6/3.8, or gemma4; "running Generative AI models on CPU is not recommended"; RAM guidance 8 GB for 7B, 16 GB for 13B, 24 GB for 33B. HA's Ollama integration defaults to `qwen3:4b-instruct`, `keep_alive=-1`, `num_ctx=8192`.

**Frigate as the camera brain (verified).** Semantic search on Jina CLIP v1 (English) or v2 (89 languages, heavier), minimum 8 GB RAM, 16 GB + GPU recommended; Coral TPUs cannot accelerate enrichments. Face recognition (since 0.16): small FaceNet on CPU or large ArcFace on GPU/NPU, min face area 750 px, thresholds detect 0.7 / unknown 0.8 / recognise 0.9, 20–30 diverse images per person, results as `sub_label`. LPR with YOLOv9 + PaddleOCR. Object descriptions triggered at end of track or after N changed frames; GenAI review summaries return structured JSON (title, scene, shortSummary, confidence 0–1, threat_level 0/1/2) and a `frigate.review_summarize` HA service. 0.17.0 added local classification training and tiered retention; 0.18 (RCs; GA date uncertain) adds a tool-calling chat agent, VLM monitoring and remote embeddings. A maintainer notes intent-style GenAI prompts were "poor and vague"; the project relies mainly on semantic search. Double Take is maintained but largely superseded by Frigate's native faces; CompreFace is Apache-2.0; InsightFace models are non-commercial. HA's LLM Vision integration adds stream analysis, data extraction to sensors, a "remember" store and a Frigate blueprint. Hardware: Frigate recommends a Beelink EQ13 (Intel N100) or Hailo-8 (7–11 ms inference).

**Smart glasses as the wearable interface.** Meta opened Ray-Ban Display (~$799, 600x600 right-lens display) to third parties via the Wearables Device Access Toolkit developer preview (14 May 2026): native Android/iOS SDKs or HTML/CSS/JS "micro-apps", camera streaming, photo, mic, audio and display access; GA publishing promised for 2026 but undated; whether third-party apps can replace Meta AI or the wake word is not stated **(uncertain)**. Meta launched $299 "Meta Glasses" June 2026 and holds ~80% of the AI-glasses market (Counterpoint, search summary). Open alternatives (verified): **MentraOS** (Apache-2.0, TypeScript SDK, apps run on the phone) supports Mentra Live, Even Realities G1/G2 and Vuzix Z100 and exposes mic transcription, camera, display and speakers; Even Realities publishes G2 app scaffolds; Brilliant Labs open-sources Frame and Halo firmware plus the Noa app. **Omi** is MIT end to end (nRF/Zephyr firmware, Flutter apps, FastAPI backend, Deepgram transcription, 24 h battery) with an ESP32-S3 camera "Omi Glass" dev kit. Recommendation: MentraOS or Even G2 for a HUD you control; treat Meta hardware as a peripheral until its toolkit is GA.

## 3. Proactive and ambient intelligence

**Mechanics available today (verified in HA source).** `assist_satellite.announce`, `start_conversation` (with `extra_system_prompt` to brief the LLM on why it is speaking; ESPHome support Mar 2025) and `ask_question` (June 2025; answers matched with hassil templates). Preannounce chime defaults true. Satellite state triggers (idle/listening/processing/responding) avoid interrupting an in-progress conversation. `ai_task.generate_data` accepts instructions, a JSON `structure` and attachments (camera snapshots), enabling scheduled briefings. ESPHome announcements time out at 5 minutes.

**Commercial patterns.** Gemini for Home's Home Brief is a daily recap of camera events with AI descriptions on the paid Advanced tier; Frigate's review summaries include `threat_level` and `confidence` as natural gating fields. Anthropic shipped scheduled tasks and Claude Code "Routines" (research preview 14 Apr 2026). OpenAI's Pulse was retired ~1 Jul 2026 into Scheduled Tasks; reviewers criticised ChatGPT Tasks as "a glorified reminder app" (search summaries). LangChain's **"ambient agents"** pattern (verified in agents-from-scratch) triages every event into ignore / notify / respond, routes actions through an Agent Inbox with accept / edit / respond / ignore, and persists feedback as memory.

**Avoiding annoyance.** Android's notification importance levels (Urgent/High/Medium/Low/None, user-owned after creation) are a useful model. Recommendations: (1) triage first, speak only for "notify"; (2) require presence (mmWave or a satellite in `idle`) before speaking; (3) quiet hours plus a per-class daily cap; (4) confidence and threat thresholds from Frigate rather than raw detections; (5) keep a "why did you say that" log via `extra_system_prompt`.

## 4. Privacy and security

**Real attacks.** SafeBreach research (published 3 Jun 2026; disclosed Aug 2025, patched Nov 2025) showed indirect prompt injection via ordinary WhatsApp/Slack/SMS notifications hijacking Gemini on Android to control Google Home devices, join Zoom calls, fake messages and poison long-term memory. "Invitation Is All You Need" (arXiv 2508.12175, Black Hat 2025) used calendar invites and emails to make Gemini control appliances, leak location, delete events and exfiltrate email (search summaries). A Jarvis that reads mail, calendars and web pages has exactly this exposure.

**Mitigations verifiable in code.** HA's OpenAI integration defaults `store_responses` off. Wyoming "has no authentication or encryption, by design" (trusted LAN only); ESPHome API encryption is enabled on Voice PE. HA backups use SecureTar (AES-128), default schedule 04:45 with jitter, retention default "keep forever". Practical rules: treat all retrieved content as data (human-in-the-loop for send/actuate), whitelist tools by satellite/room, spoken confirmation for locks, garage, boiler and payments, log every tool call.

**Data retention at API providers (Aug 2026, search summaries; verify contracts).** OpenAI announced "Private Safety Processing" (19–20 Aug 2026) to preserve Zero Data Retention for eligible enterprise/API customers. Anthropic deletes API inputs/outputs within 30 days, with ZDR agreements available; 30-day retention is required for certain frontier models (Fable-tier) per Anthropic docs. Google does not train on paid Gemini API/Vertex data; ZDR-equivalent terms exist for Vertex via DPA amendment; consumer Gemini retains 72 hours even with activity off.

**Law and household etiquette.** EDPB Guidelines 02/2021 on virtual voice assistants apply GDPR and ePrivacy to VVAs; voice becomes biometric data when used for speaker identification. GDPR Recital 18's household exemption covers purely personal recording but is lost when data is shared or used commercially; the EU AI Act prohibits certain real-time biometric identification (Article 5) and imposes transparency duties on AI that interacts with people (search summaries; obtain legal advice for your jurisdiction). Hardware: Voice PE's side switch (GPIO3) physically cuts mic power, turns the LED red and cannot be overridden from software; Satellite1 offers hardware and software mute. Recommendation: a guest mode that disables person recognition, memory writes and email/calendar tools; a child profile with no purchases or door control; speaker identification only with explicit enrolment (no HA core speaker-ID PRs exist as of Sept 2026).

## 5. Reliability and operations

**Watchdogs (verified).** HA Supervisor pings the Core API every 120 s and restarts Core after 2 consecutive misses, escalates through up to 5 "reanimate" attempts then safe mode; container restarts limited to 5 attempts at 10 s spacing, throttled to 10 per 30 minutes; add-on watchdogs restart stopped add-ons. ESPHome: ESP32 task watchdog default 5 s, `safe_mode` after 10 failed boots, OTA on dual partitions with MD5, optional signed images, downgrade protection and rollback; 2026.8.1 added OTA retry on transient errors; 2026.9 betas add lost-chunk retransmission. Voice PE firmware ships roughly monthly (26.6.0 on 18 Jun 2026). Cautionary tale: HA Core 2026.3.2 broke Voice PE wake-word handling and the fix was rollback to 2026.3.1, so pin and stage updates.

**Graceful degradation.** Keep a fully local path: microWakeWord on device, Speech-to-Phrase (Kaldi FST, 21 languages) or streaming ASR, hassil plus the gazetteer matcher, local TTS. "Prefer handling commands locally" had regression reports in 2025.3, so test the local path after every upgrade. Keep the LLM resident (`keep_alive=-1`) and cap history (HA default 20 messages / 1 hour).

**Power and cost (blog estimates; measure your own).** A 24/7 RTX 4090 box idles at ~30–50 W GPU-side, ~$132/year whole-system at US average rates; full load ~$52/month at $0.12/kWh. Capping a 4090 from 450 W to 300 W reportedly costs only 5–10% tokens/s because inference is memory-bandwidth bound. Put the GPU box in a separate room; use an N100 mini PC for Frigate.

## 6. Reference implementations and products to learn from

- **Home Assistant / Open Home Foundation**: Voice PE ($59, XMOS, hardware mute), linux-voice-assistant, Satellite1 (open XMOS AEC firmware, mmWave presence), speech-to-phrase, gazetteer matcher, ai_task, streaming TTS. Lessons: local-first fallback and explicit conversation-start APIs; weaknesses: punctuation-based follow-up detection and self-hearing bugs.
- **Amazon Alexa+**: US rollout Feb 2026, free with Prime or $19.99/month; reviews report 30-second waits, "unbearably erratic" behaviour, half-completed tasks (Wired, Consumer Reports, TechRadar; search summaries). Lesson: latency and reliability regressions destroy trust faster than new features earn it.
- **Google Gemini for Home**: launched Oct 2025; Gemini 3.1 spring update, continued conversation Apr 2026, Home Brief/Ask Home on the paid tier, new Google Home Speaker (June 2026). Lesson: camera intelligence and daily briefings are the proactive features users pay for.
- **Apple Siri**: Gemini-powered Siri confirmed 22 Apr 2026; WWDC (8 Jun 2026) showed the rebuilt assistant with on-screen awareness and personal context, two years after the 2024 demo. Lesson: do not announce anticipatory features before they ship.
- **OpenAI/Jony Ive device**: nothing ships before end of Feb 2027 per a Feb 2026 court filing; screenless or nearly so; reported blockers are always-on privacy, compute and "personality" (uncertain).
- **Rabbit R1 / Humane Ai Pin**: Humane discontinued; Rabbit teases 2027 hardware. Lesson: devices that tried to replace the phone failed; narrow, reliable use cases survived.
- **Friend vs Omi pendants**: Friend ($99) reviewed as barely able to hear and 7–10 s laggy; Omi (open source, opt-in capture, developer extensibility, local backend option) shows the viable version.

**Overall recommendations.** Build on Home Assistant's satellite/pipeline APIs for the home surface and MentraOS or Even G2 for the wearable surface; hit latency with local intents plus streamed LLM audio and mandatory fillers; give the system eyes through Frigate (faces, semantic search, structured review summaries) feeding a local Qwen3-VL/Gemma 4 via llama.cpp, with Gemini Live at 1 fps only when explicitly asked to look; make proactivity a triage-notify-confirm pipeline with presence gating and per-class caps; assume every email, page and notification is hostile and require spoken confirmation for actuation; run behind Supervisor/ESPHome watchdogs, staged updates, encrypted off-site backups, and a power-limited resident GPU.
